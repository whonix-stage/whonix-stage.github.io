Donate with PayPal

On PayPal's donation page you can choose the amount and a one-time or monthly donation, and pay with a PayPal balance, credit or debit card, or many other payment options. No PayPal account is required for many options.

PayPal versus Tor

Browser specific.

  • A) Most browser (such as Mozilla Firefox or Chrome): No special notice.
  • B) Tor Browser:
    • If using Tor or Tor Browser, at time of writing PayPal was blocking connections from the Tor network.
    • PayPal very most likely requires use of a clearnet browser.
    • This is a decision by PayPal and unspecific to Whonix.
    • For alternatives, consider for example donate by bank wire or donate by crypto currency.
