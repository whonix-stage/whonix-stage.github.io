AI agents are restricted on the Whonix website.

Some AI Agent Actions are Restricted

Currently all HTTP POST actions (wiki editing and forum posting) are blocked. This is due to spam. If you have a legitimate reason using an AI agent, please discuss this in the forums.

Read access is still permissible. Content scraping and AI training is still permissible.
