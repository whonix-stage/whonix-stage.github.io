Anyone interested in using these elements is better off acquiring them from the origin/upstream and not from Whonix, since Whonix does not fork it. This description is purposefully generic in nature. The actual current license (version) of MediaWiki is deliberately unmentioned. This avoids inaccuracies and publications becoming outdated at a later time. The reader is recommended to seek this information from the original MediaWiki source.
